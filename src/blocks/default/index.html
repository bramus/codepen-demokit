<calc-input>

A custom input element that accepts mathematical formulas such as 2 + 3 or (2 + 3) * 4. Upon blurring, it displays the calculated result (e.g. 5 or 20); upon re-focusing, it reveals the entered formula again.

Interactive Formula & 3-Inputs Inspector

State: Blurred (Showing Result)

Click or tab into the input below to reveal the formula. Under the hood, three <input type="text"> elements stay synchronized while only one is visible at a time.
